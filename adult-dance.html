Adult dance

Adult Ballet
& Contemporary

These sessions bring together dance technique, musicality and creative movement with a more embodied, somatic approach. We work with the foundations of ballet and contemporary dance while also exploring breath, sensation, weight, space and the natural intelligence of the body.

Classes offer a chance to develop strength, coordination, flexibility and confidence, while leaving room for curiosity, expression and individuality. You don’t need to be a professional dancer, or even have extensive experience. Whether you’re returning to dance, continuing a long-standing practice, or simply want to move in a deeper and more creative way, you are welcome.

Expect thoughtful teaching, beautiful music, a little challenge, and space to discover how you want to move.
